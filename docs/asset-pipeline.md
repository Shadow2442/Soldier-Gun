# Asset Pipeline

## Art

Create original assets that echo early-90s console shooter clarity without copying source games.

### Sprite targets

- Player ship at small-screen scale with readable wings and engine glow
- Enemy families grouped by stage biome and behavior
- Bullets designed for instant contrast
- HUD with crisp, compact typography

### Production notes

- Work from a limited palette first
- Test every sprite at native scale in motion
- Prefer strong silhouette over fine detail
- Keep animation counts small and purposeful

## Audio

Aim for a bright, driving soundtrack inspired by PC Engine-era PSG/FM texture.

### Music direction

- Melodic leads
- Tight bass movement
- Punchy synthetic drums
- Short loop-friendly arrangements

### SFX direction

- Fast, punchy player shots
- Distinct weapon pickup tones
- Dense but not harsh explosions
- Clear bomb activation cue

## File conventions

- Music exports go in `assets/audio/music/`
- Sound effects go in `assets/audio/sfx/`
- Source files can live under `assets/source/`
